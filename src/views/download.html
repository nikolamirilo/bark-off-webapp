Before your first session.

BarkOff walks you through setup in the app. These four things make the biggest difference.

  • Record both messagesOne gentle, one firm. BarkOff needs both before it starts.
  • Turn the volume upYour dog needs to hear you from across the room.
  • Turn off auto-lockSo the phone keeps listening while you are out.
  • Plug it inThe screen stays on, so a charger helps on long days.
