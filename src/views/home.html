    Features

    Built for the hours you are away.

    Hears barks, not the house

    BarkOff keeps track of how loud your room usually is. A sound has to rise well above that, start sharply, and last long enough to count.

    • Real barksSharp start, at least 0.15 seconds long
    • Door clicks and tapsOver too fast to be a bark
    • TV, fans and trafficNo sharp start, so they become the new normal

    Two levels, two messages

    Whining and soft barks get your gentle message. Loud barking gets the firm one.

    Soft barkBig bark

    Calibrate by barking

    Stay quiet, then bark. BarkOff measures your room and the bark, then sets the level with a little headroom.

    Cooldown you control

    Pick 5 to 30 seconds between messages. It always stays longer than your longest clip.

    See when barking happened, how loud it got, and how it compares with last time.

    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Still wondering about something? Send us a message

    Do I need a second phone?
    You need a phone that stays at home with your dog, and an old phone works well. Keep it plugged in for long sessions, because the screen stays on while BarkOff listens.
    Will the TV or traffic set it off?
    Usually not. BarkOff tracks how loud your room normally is and only reacts to sounds with the quick, sharp start of a bark. Steady noise and short clicks are ignored. You can try it in the Bark Lab above.
    What does my dog hear?
    Your voice. You record a gentle message for soft barking and a firmer one for loud barking. A cooldown of 5 to 30 seconds keeps the messages from repeating too often.
    Does BarkOff record my dog?
    It measures loudness to spot barks. That audio is never played back or uploaded, and it is deleted when the session ends. Reports keep only the numbers, like the time and loudness of each bark.
    Which phones does it work on?
    Android phones with Android 7.1 or newer. You can download BarkOff from this site today, and it is coming to Google Play soon. BarkOff is not available for iPhone.
    Why does Android warn me when I install it?
    Android shows a warning for any app that does not come from Google Play, so it is expected while BarkOff is a direct download. Allow the install, and only download BarkOff from this site.
    How much does it cost?
    Nothing. BarkOff is free. If you like it, you can support the team with a coffee.
    Can I change the sensitivity later?
    Yes. Move the sliders or calibrate again in Settings whenever you like, for example after moving the phone to another room.
