    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Still wondering about something? Send us a message

    Do I need a second phone?
    You need a phone that stays at home with your dog, and an old phone works well. Keep it plugged in for long sessions, because the screen stays on while BarkOff listens.
    Will the TV or traffic set it off?
    Usually not. BarkOff tracks how loud your room normally is and only reacts to sounds with the quick, sharp start of a bark. Steady noise and short clicks are ignored. You can try it in the Bark Lab above.
    What does my dog hear?
    Your voice. You record a gentle message for soft barking and a firmer one for loud barking. A cooldown of 5 to 30 seconds keeps the messages from repeating too often.
    Does BarkOff record my dog?
    It measures loudness to spot barks. That audio is never played back or uploaded, and it is deleted when the session ends. Reports keep only the numbers, like the time and loudness of each bark.
    Which phones does it work on?
    Android 7.1 or newer, in early access now. iPhone is coming soon, and you can join the testing group to hear when it is ready.
    How much does it cost?
    BarkOff is free during early access. If you like it, you can support the team with a coffee.
    Can I change the sensitivity later?
    Yes. Move the sliders or calibrate again in Settings whenever you like, for example after moving the phone to another room.

    Smart detection

    It knows a bark from the rest of the house.

    A simple volume limit is either too deaf in a quiet bedroom or always going off next to a TV. BarkOff measures the room first, then looks at the shape of each sound.

    • Checks the sound 20 times a second
    • Learns your room's normal noise level and keeps adjusting it
    • Needs a sharp rise and at least 0.15 seconds of sound
    • Starts your message while your dog is still barking

    Each chart shows loudness over two seconds. The dashed line is the trigger, 14 dB above the room.

    Two levels, two messages

    A soft voice for whining. A firm one for barking.

    BarkOff sorts every bark into one of two levels and plays the message you recorded for it. A good rule: make the first one soothing and the second one calm but firm.

    Soft bark Whining, grumbles, small woofs
    "Hey Max, it is okay. I will be home soon."
    Big bark Loud, full barking
    "Max, quiet now. Good dog."
    Calibration

    Set the level by barking at your phone.

    Not sure where to put the slider? Let BarkOff measure it. One short test gives it both the quiet room and a bark to compare.

    Hold the phone where it will sit during the day, at the same distance from your dog.

    1. Stay quietBarkOff listens to the room for a moment.
    2. Bark onceOr let your dog do it. Your best impression works too.
    3. DoneThe level is set just below that bark, with a little headroom.
    Cooldown

    Room to settle between messages.

    After a message plays, BarkOff waits before it plays another. Barks during that time still show up in your report.

    • Choose 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 seconds
    • Always longer than your longest message, so they never overlap

    Privacy

    Privacy notes.

    What BarkOff does with sound and data, in plain words. Last updated October 2, 2026.

    Draft for review. Please have this checked before the site goes live.

    The short version

    BarkOff listens for barks on your phone, plays the messages you record, and keeps your data on your phone. We do not sell personal data.

    Microphone

    BarkOff uses the microphone only while a listening session or a calibration is running. It measures loudness to spot barks. That audio is processed on your phone, never played back or uploaded, and deleted when the session ends.

    Your voice messages

    The messages you record are saved on your phone and only play there. Deleting a message, or the app, removes them.

    Pet profile and reports

    Your dog's name and photo, and your session reports, are stored on your phone. Reports hold numbers only, such as the time and loudness of each bark. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

    App analytics

    We use Vexo Analytics to learn how the app is used, for example when a session starts or ends, how long it ran and how many barks were counted. These events never include audio or voice recordings.

    This website

    • When you use a form on this site, we receive what you type in it, such as your name, email and message.
    • We use it only to reply to you and to run the testing group.
    • To add you as a tester, we enter your email in the Google Play Console.

    Your choices

    You can ask us to delete anything you sent through this site. Email us at barkoffapp@gmail.com.
